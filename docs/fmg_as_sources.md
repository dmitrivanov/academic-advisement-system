## Footnote translations

- `FMG_AS_MATH_QUANT` keeps the complete Mathematical and Quantitative
  Reasoning pool and displays the strong MAT 206 recommendation.
- `FMG_AS_INDIVIDUAL` keeps the complete Individual and Society pool and
  displays the strong ECO 202 recommendation.
- `FMG_AS_US_EXPERIENCE` keeps the complete U.S. Experience pool and displays
  the strong ECO 201 recommendation.
- `FMG_AS_CREATIVE` excludes SPE 100 and SPE 102 from the second Creative
  Expression slot because one speech course is separately required.
- `FMG_AS_GENERAL_ELECTIVE` keeps the complete general-elective pool, explains
  that Common Core STEM excess may satisfy some of the five credits, and
  recommends MAT 209, ACC 222, or CIS 200.
- The published cap of no more than two Flexible Core courses in one discipline
  spans multiple independent groups. The current rule engine cannot enforce a
  cross-group subject-count cap, so it is documented rather than silently
  approximated.

## Prerequisites and equivalencies

- FNB 100: the live page lists MAT 051 or an Elementary Algebra exemption.
  Neither is a degree-level requirement on the supplied map, and the system
  cannot record a placement exemption, so it remains an advising check rather
  than a machine lock that exempt students could never clear.
- FNB 230 and FNB 300: FNB 100 and ACC 122.
- FNB 250: FNB 100, ECO 100, ECO 201, or ECO 202. ECO 250 is modeled as the
  same-course alternative.
- MAT 301: MAT 206 or MAT 206.5.

## Degree-map sequence

The registered map follows the official semester targets of 13, 16, 15, and
16 credits. The map visually shows four General Elective credits because its
MAT 206 STEM variant contributes one excess credit; the curriculum requirement
remains five credits and the selector explains the permitted STEM-excess route.
A Writing Intensive course is also required for graduation.
