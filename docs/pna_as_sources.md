## Footnote translations

- `PNA_AS_MATH_QUANT` contains MAT 150 and the map's explicit MAT 206
  alternate. The student-facing note explains that the appropriate math path
  depends on the intended transfer institution.
- SPE 100 and SPE 102 are alternatives. SPE 102 is the option identified for
  non-native speakers of English.
- ECO 201 is a literal Flexible Core course satisfying U.S. Experience in Its
  Diversity, exactly as shown on the map.
- `PNA_AS_CREATIVE` excludes SPE 100 and SPE 102 from the second Creative
  Expression slot because one speech course is already required.
- `PNA_AS_PROGRAM_ELECTIVES` requires three courses / nine credits from BUS
  150, BUS 200, ECO 225, MAR 100, PAN 240, or PAN 250.
- `PNA_AS_GENERAL_ELECTIVE` provides a complete general-elective selector and
  explains that Common Core STEM excess may satisfy part of the six credits.
- The published cap of no more than two Flexible Core courses in one discipline
  spans multiple independent groups. It is documented because the current rule
  engine cannot enforce a cross-group subject-count cap.

## Prerequisites

- PAN 100 is the prerequisite for PAN 230 and every 200-level PAN elective.
- BUS 150 requires ENG 101, ENG 201, and BUS 104.
- ECO 225 requires ECO 201 or ECO 202.

## Degree-map sequence

The registered map follows the official semester targets of 16, 15, 15, and
14 credits. The map shows five visible General Elective credits because MAT 150
contributes one excess credit; the curriculum requirement remains six credits.
A Writing Intensive course is required for graduation.
