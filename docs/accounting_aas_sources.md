## Curriculum decisions

- The program is modeled as `ACCT_AAS`, 60 credits, catalog 2025-2026.
- The 14-credit Required Common Core contains ENG 101, ENG 201, one of MAT
  150/MAT 206, and one of AST 110/PHY 110.
- The six-credit Flexible Core contains one of SPE 100/SPE 102 and an
  `ACCT_MEDIA_ARTS` selector narrowed to Pathways-approved ART and MUS
  courses.
- ECO 201/ECO 202 and CIS 100/CIS 200 are true OR requirements. ECO 202
  requires MAT 206. CIS 200 models ACC 122 as the required prior ACC/BUS
  course; the separate computer competency test cannot be represented as a
  completed college course and remains in the advising note.
- `ACCT_HEALTH` accepts three-credit HSD courses and ACL 205, matching the
  current requirements page. The supplied map shows the HSD route.
- The Accounting elective is a three-credit pool containing exactly ACC 150,
  ACC 242, ACC 331, ACC 360, and ACC 370, with published course
  prerequisites.
- CED 301 departmental permission and the graduation-wide Writing Intensive
  requirement remain advising notes rather than selectable courses.
