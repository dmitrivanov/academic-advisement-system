## Program-specific choice rules

- MAT 206 satisfies Mathematical and Quantitative Reasoning.
- The first science is restricted to BIO 210, CHE 201, PHY 210, or PHY 215.
- The Scientific World selection is restricted to BIO 220, CHE 202, CSC 110,
  CSC 111, PHY 220, or PHY 225.
- SPE 100 or SPE 102 satisfies one Creative Expression requirement.
- The second Creative Expression course cannot be SPE 100 or SPE 102.
- Four program electives totaling at least 12 credits are required.
- A Writing Intensive course is required for graduation.

## Modeling notes

The published group totals equal 60 credits. MAT 206 and the required science
options are four-credit STEM variants in three-credit Pathways categories. The
official program explains that excess Common Core STEM credits can satisfy the
three-credit General Elective requirement.

The current progress model displays that final requirement as `General Elective or
Common Core STEM excess credits`. Automatic cross-group surplus-credit application
is not yet represented, so students should confirm this item with an advisor.

The validator warnings for group listed-credit totals and external prerequisites
are expected: they describe elective pools, four-credit STEM variants, and approved
alternatives rather than errors in the published 60-credit requirement structure.
