## Representation notes

The CSV transcribes the courses and credit-bearing open categories printed on the selected official two-year map. Named prerequisites that the current schema can express are included where reviewed. Corequisites, term-only availability, permissions, minimum grades, repeated-course rules, and specialization branches cannot be represented by the current schema and remain in the official footnotes below. Program-specific open elective pools use the existing broad selector only as a visible selection mechanism and require advisor verification.
