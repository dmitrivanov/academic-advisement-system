# CUNY Beyond Phase 9 - Simple Tester Guide

## What A Student Can Do Now

A high-school student or other visitor can open CUNY Beyond without logging in, browse all reviewed careers, choose a job goal, select their skills, and receive a sourced BMCC major recommendation. The app explains why the major appears and warns when a profession normally needs more education or a license.

## Test 1 - Browse Instead of Guessing

1. Open `/cuny-beyond`.
2. Choose **High-school student** and continue.
3. Open **Browse all reviewed careers**.
4. Type `accounting` in **Filter careers**.
5. Choose **Accounting Clerk**.
6. Continue with **Currently working: No**.
7. Choose **Working with numbers** and at least one other skill.
8. Choose **None of these** for prior learning.
9. Click **Find my BMCC program matches**.

Expected result: Accounting A.A.S. appears with an official BMCC source and an explanation tied to entry-level accounting preparation.

## Test 2 - Type A Common Alias

Repeat the flow with career goal `bookkeeper`.

Expected result: the app reports that it matched the reviewed **Accounting Clerk** profile and recommends Accounting A.A.S.

## Test 3 - Human Services

Use career goal `case worker` and choose **Helping people**, **Communicating ideas**, and **Organizing projects**.

Expected result: the canonical match is **Case Manager** and Human Services A.S. appears.

## Test 4 - Criminal Justice

Use career goal `law enforcement`.

Expected result: the canonical match is **Police Officer** and Criminal Justice A.A. appears.

## Test 5 - Urban Studies

Use career goal `city planner`.

Expected result: the canonical match is **Urban Planner** and Urban Studies A.A. appears.

## Test 6 - One Career With Multiple Starting Points

Use career goal `Mental Health Counselor` and select helping and communication skills.

Expected result: Human Services A.S. and Psychology General A.A. can both appear. Each card explains that professional practice may require additional education or licensure.

## Test 7 - Responsive And Keyboard Access

1. Resize the browser to a narrow phone width.
2. Open the career browser and confirm it becomes one column.
3. Use Tab to reach the filter and career buttons.
4. Type a filter and activate a result using Enter or Space.
5. Confirm the visible result count updates.

## Test 8 - Unknown Goal Recovery

Enter a goal that is not in the reviewed catalog and complete the flow.

Expected result: the app does not invent a match. It asks the student to browse supported careers or explore the goal with an advisor.

## Important Interpretation

A recommended major is a reviewed starting point, not a promise of a job or professional eligibility. Follow the linked official program source and confirm transfer, licensing, and career requirements with the appropriate advisor.
